CYMBAL ON WEBSITE

TERMS

Cymbal is a hobby app for friends, provided as it is, with no promise that it will always be available or always get a song right.

POSTING

  • Post links to single songs on Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube. Albums, playlists, podcasts, channels and short links aren't accepted.
  • Posting tries to add the song to all three of the owner's "Cymbal on Website" playlists. The link you posted is always the one that counts; the other two services get the same recording only when Cymbal can find it with confidence.
  • When it can't be sure (a live version, a remix, two recordings that look alike), the song is marked for attention instead of guessing, and the owner can fix it with an exact link.
  • Be decent in captions and comments. The owner can remove anything.
  • There are limits: 10 posts and 60 comments per person per hour.

THE PLAYLISTS

  • The playlists belong to the owner's own Spotify, YouTube and Apple Music accounts, which the owner has authorised Cymbal to add to. Friends never connect an account.
  • Spotify and YouTube are updated in the background, usually within a few minutes. YouTube has a daily allowance; if it runs out, songs wait until the next day.
  • Apple Music only allows additions from the owner's own authorised browser, so Apple additions wait until the owner next opens Cymbal there, and then catch up on their own.
  • Playlists are append-only in this version: removing a post does not remove a song that has already been added.
  • Songs are identified using each service's own catalogue and MusicBrainz. Cymbal doesn't copy, stream or store audio, and doesn't use an AI model to match songs.

THE SERVICES

Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube are used under their own terms, and each can change or withdraw what Cymbal relies on. If a service's terms stop allowing something Cymbal does, the owner switches that part off.
