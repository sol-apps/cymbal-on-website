CYMBAL ON WEBSITE

TERMS

Cymbal is a hobby app, provided as it is, with no promise that it will always be up or always pick the right song.

POSTING

  • Post links to single songs on Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube. Albums, playlists, podcasts, channels and short links aren't accepted.
  • Each post is tried on all three of the owner's "Cymbal on Website" playlists. When Cymbal can't be sure it has the same recording, it doesn't guess; the owner can fix it by hand.
  • Be decent. The owner can remove anything.
  • There are limits: 10 posts and 60 comments an hour from one browser, and more generous ones per network.

THE PLAYLISTS

  • They belong to the owner's own Spotify, YouTube and Apple Music accounts.
  • Spotify and YouTube update in the background. YouTube has a daily allowance; if it runs out, songs wait until the next day. Apple Music catches up when the owner next opens Cymbal.
  • They only grow: removing a post doesn't remove a song already added.
  • Songs are matched using each service's own catalogue and MusicBrainz. Cymbal doesn't copy, stream or store audio, and doesn't use AI to match songs.
