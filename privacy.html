CYMBAL ON WEBSITE

PRIVACY

Cymbal has no accounts. Anyone with the link can read it and post under whatever name they type.

WHAT IS STORED

  • Each post: the link, the name and caption you typed, the track's title, artist, length and ISRC, and when it was posted.
  • Each comment: the name and text you typed, and when.
  • A random key your browser makes the first time you visit. Cymbal keeps a scrambled copy (a hash) so it can tell that a post came from your browser and let you remove it. Clearing your browser data loses it.
  • A scrambled copy of your network address, used only for rate limits. The address itself isn't kept.
  • For the owner only: the authorisation that lets Cymbal add songs to the owner's Spotify and YouTube playlists, stored encrypted.

WHAT LEAVES CYMBAL

To find a song on the other services, Cymbal sends track identifiers, titles, artist names and ISRCs to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube and MusicBrainz. Never your name, caption or comments.

The three playlists are public and contain songs only.

REMOVING THINGS

You can remove your own posts and comments from the browser you wrote them in; the owner can remove anything. Removing blanks the text straight away. A song already on a playlist stays there. Data is kept on the solhann.net server and its week of nightly backups.
